# Packing

You know what you own - this is the list to tick against. **Bold = specific to this trip and easy to forget.**

**Bag rule**: checked 20 kg holds the rod tube (44-47 cm), gas-adjacent kit and anything heavy. The free 40x30x20 under-seat bag has **no weight limit**, one each - put the densest things there.

## Cannot buy there

- [ ] **Gas canister is NOT in the bag** - flights ban it. Buy in Shkoder Monday, fallback the EUR 12 locker
- [ ] **Nothing extra for fishing.** Sea and lake are dropped; Theth is dry flies, which you already have
- [x] **Water filter** - Khrystyna brings it. Drought year, spring water is not guaranteed
- [x] **A card game** - ordered, must land by Fri 11
- [x] **Head torches, both of you** - rechargeable, charge them full before you fly. Sat night walk back from the dunes, and two wild camp nights
- [ ] **Water shoes or old trainers** for the Kir canyon (glass reported) and the river pools
- [ ] **Cash in EUR to change** - card acceptance is thin outside Tirana and Shkoder; Blue Eye and canyon parking are cash-only lek

## Camp

- [ ] tent, pegs, footprint, **sleeping bags good to ~4 C comfort** - that is the forecast night at the high camp, not a guess about altitude
- [ ] sleeping mats, pillows
- [ ] stove, pot, lighter **plus a spare lighter**, spork each, knife
- [ ] 2x 2 L bladder or bottles each, **plus empty capacity for the 8 L Thursday carry**
- [ ] headtorches, powerbank, cables
- [ ] rubbish bags - pack everything out
- [ ] small trowel, toilet paper

## Hike

- [ ] packs: her Montis 60+10, your Tatonka ~70 L
- [ ] boots worn on the plane, sandals for camp and rivers
- [ ] **rain shell each.** No storm is forecast, but you are out for three days at altitude and afternoon showers are normal here
- [ ] **dry bag or bin liner inside each pack.** Sleeping bags and the warm layer must reach camp dry; a wet bag at 2,206 m is the one failure with no recovery
- [ ] warm layer + hat + gloves for the 2,206 m night, and **something dry to change into** after Friday
- [ ] poles if you have them - the Friday descent is 1,860 m down
- [ ] **offline map downloaded, forecast cached** before Theth loses signal
- [ ] first aid, blister kit, tape
- [ ] sun cream, sunglasses, cap

## Beach and water

- [ ] swimwear, quick-dry towels
- [ ] **snorkel mask** - the rocky headland Maja e Zeze north of camp drops to 2-3 m over rock
- [ ] frisbee or ball for the sand
- [ ] **charcoal + firelighters** - buy at the Spar, do not count on driftwood

## Fishing

- [ ] 9 ft 6 wt, floating line, leaders, tippet
- [ ] dry flies (Theth trout - the real fishing)
- [ ] nippers, forceps, floatant
- [ ] **no licence needed** anywhere from the bank in Albania

## Documents and admin

- [ ] passports, driving licence, EHIC or insurance
- [ ] **car booking + EUR 69.47 due at the desk**, deposit 600 held
- [ ] **check your phone plan covers Albania** - outside the EU, standard roaming may not apply. The whole navigation and safety plan assumes data
- [ ] **check in online TWICE, both are easy to miss:**
  - **outbound: opens Fri 11 Sep 11:50** - 24 h before the flight out. Do it while you pack
  - **return: opens Fri 18 Sep 13:50** - you are driving to Tirana then with no signal, so do it from the flat that evening
  - miss either and Ryanair charges you at the airport
