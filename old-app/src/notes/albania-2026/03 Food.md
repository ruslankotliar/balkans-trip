# Food

Three shops matter. **Conad in Shkoder on Monday is the one that cannot go wrong** - after it there is no supermarket until Friday night. **The Wed-Fri loop food is its own page: [[Hiking food]]** - what to buy in Vienna, the day-by-day meals and calories, the Conad shopping list.

`Albania: an inexpensive restaurant is ~1,000 lek, EUR 9-10. A main plus salad and a drink 1,000-1,200 lek`

## Sat 12 - Spar, Lezhe, `07:00-22:00`

For the fire tonight and breakfast tomorrow.

- **sausages** - the easy fire food, no prep, no pan
- **meat for the coals**: chicken thighs or pork neck, already cut
- **veg that survives a fire**: corn on the cob, peppers, courgette, onion, potatoes in foil
- **foil** - the thing everyone forgets
- bread, tomatoes, cucumber, feta, olives, olive oil
- **charcoal + firelighters**
- water, 6+ L
- beer or wine
- breakfast: eggs, bread, coffee or tea
- **cash from the ATM here**

## Sun 13 - butcher in Shkoder, for the BBQ at the house

**Shpija e Mishit**, Bulevardi Skenderbeu 54, in the bazaar cluster. Buy on the way back from the canyon, not in the morning - meat should not sit in a hot car all day.

- **qofte** and **lamb** are the standard grill buy here
- Tani Mini-Market in Shiroka `08:00-20:00`, 0.9 km, for anything missed. ATM beside it

## Mon 14 - Conad, Shkoder, `07:00-21:00`

**Everything for Theth and all three loop days. Last supermarket.** Shopping list is on [[Hiking food]].

**Conad is in the plan for its position, not its price - it is the last supermarket before Theth.** Spar and Big Market are cheaper for the same goods, so use one if you pass it in Shkoder. **Fruit and veg from a street stall or the bazaar either way.**

**Bread from a bakery, not the supermarket shelf** - cheaper and still warm.
