## Shopping list, Conad/Spar Shkoder Monday

| | |
|---|---|
| **Calories per gram** | nuts, cured sausage, hard cheese, chocolate, olive oil, peanut butter - this is where the weight pays |
| **Bulk** | pasta, couscous (no cooking, just hot water), instant rice, instant mash |
| **Breakfast** | oats, powdered milk, honey, coffee sachets, a few tea bags |
| **On the move** | dried fruit, energy bars, salted nuts - Friday's descent burns as much as Wednesday's climb |
| **Flavour** | stock cubes, salt, a tube of tomato paste, garlic |
| **Something sweet** | a bar of local halva or Turkish delight, on top of the trail chocolate |
| **Do not bring** | anything with water in it - tins, fresh veg beyond one onion |
