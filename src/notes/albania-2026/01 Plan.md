## Fishing

**All of it is the Shala river in Theth. Dawn and last light, nowhere else.** No sea, no lake - one evening at each is not enough to be worth the gear.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Mon 17:30-19:00** | Ura e Gerles, walk from the tent |
| **Tue 06:00-08:15** | same stretch, dawn. The best window of the week |
| **Tue 18:00-19:00** | village stretch, **or Nderlysa if Monday fished badly** |

Nderlysa is the same river, 6 km down-valley, bigger water. Narrow paved road, 15 min, fine in the dark taken slowly. Wed to Fri is above the fish.

### The canyon - three pools and a cave, all on the same road

|                | Park                       | Walk                            | What                                       |
| -------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Pool One**   | car park, 200 lek          | 5 min                           | the busy one, deep, wooden bridge in       |
| **Pool Two**   | layby, room for two cars   | 1 min                           | quiet, shallow entry then deep immediately |
| **Pool Three** | free, marked as a campsite | 1 min, best pool 100 m upstream | **the jump rock locals use**               |
| **Zhyle cave** | its own car park           | easy scramble                   | deep pool inside, ice cold. **Torch**      |

- **Depth-check every jump yourself. Levels change and 2026 is a drought year.**
- **Water shoes.** Broken glass has been found at Pool Three.
- **Leave the rod in the car.** This canyon is close to fishless.

**FISH - Shiroka promenade, dusk.** Perch on a small streamer, straight off the house's doorstep. Confirmed species, nobody has confirmed catching one on a fly. 30-60 min, no travel.

**SUP / kayak** - Drini Times, in Shiroka. Walk past, see if they hand you a board. Flat lake, low priority, no loss if shut.

### The evening at the house

- **Cards and dice** - buy before you fly. Durak, Gin Rummy, Cribbage. Yahtzee needs 5 dice and paper
- **Music** - [[Playlist]], downloaded offline
- **Wine or raki** - from a shop, not a bar

## Wed 16 - the big climb, over Qafa e Pejes

`first light 05:53, sunset 18:50`

**[The route, both camps in it](https://www.komoot.com/tour/3244329907) - 27.8 km, +2,183 m over three days.**

- **Away by 07:00.** 10.1 km, +1,078 m, 825 -> 1,673 m
- Water: **km 4.5 and 5.8**, Burimi i Shtrazes, ~40 m off the line
- **km 8.4 Peja Lake - assume it is dry.** Nice to be wrong
- **km 10.1 White Spring camp, 1,673 m.** Spring 16 m from the pitch, plus a ford. The only water on this route anyone has actually seen
- In camp early afternoon. Long cold evening at 1,673 m
- **Pitch for drainage, not just for flat.** Severe afternoon thunderstorms were logged here in late August

This is the hard day - the "alpine experience" rating on the route is the Peja climb, not the Valbona side.

## Thu 17 - short day, but you carry the water up

`sunset 18:49`

- 6.1 km, +710 m, 1,673 -> 2,206 m
- **km 14.0 Burimi Gropave te Bukura, 6 m off the line, 1,740 m. FILL EVERYTHING. 8 litres for two.** **Nobody has confirmed this one runs** - the whole high camp depends on it
- Then 2.2 km and +466 m carrying it. **There is nothing at 2,206 m** - no water within 2.1 km
- **km 16.2 the high camp, 2,206 m** - the most remote pitch on the loop, 1.7 km from the nearest building

### The Thursday call, and it is a real one

**Only if Monday shows a real, agreed-on wet Thursday or Friday** - not one alarming number - **skip the 2,206 m night.** Push over instead: km 14.0 -> the [Valbona pass](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=42.4074,19.8143) 1,780 m -> camp on the **meadow at km 21.5, ~1,750 m**. **`Re-checked 2026-09-11`: two of three models now put the rain on Thursday itself, not Friday - see the Weather table above. Watch this Monday.**

| | The high camp, km 16.2 | Over the pass, km 21.5 |
|---|---|---|
| Thursday | 6.1 km, easy | **11.4 km, the pass in it** |
| **Friday** | **11.6 km down in the rain** | **6.3 km down** |
| Water | 8 L carried, on an unseen spring | **Burimet e Valbones, 300 m away** |
| Night | 4 C, most remote pitch on the loop | 4 C, toilets and two trail bars within 1.1 km |
| | your own komoot find, properly wild | 818 m off the line, not wild |

**It trades the wildest night of the trip for a short wet Friday and no water gamble.** **As it stands today, take the high camp.** Only reopen it if Monday genuinely changes the picture.

**If the km 14 spring is dry** - and 2026 is a drought year - do not go up. Camp at Gropa e Bukur (1,740 m) or push over the pass to Burimet e Valbones at km 21.2. Decide there, with water in your hands.

**Not sure? Fill 8 L at White Spring (km 10.1) instead** and carry it 3.9 km further. Heavy Thursday morning, no gamble.
