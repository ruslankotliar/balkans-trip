# Playlist

**Your own bookmarks are in [[Music]] - use those for the drives.** This page is artists you do NOT already have, picked to sit next to what you listen to. **My picks, not yours - cut anything that misses.**

`Artists are real and I know their work. I have not checked individual track titles, so search by artist and let the top tracks play.`

## Drives - next to Worakls, N'to, Ross M

Melodic and organic house, the same emotional register, all with long DJ sets on YouTube and Spotify.

**Ben Bohmer** - the closest thing to Worakls that is not Worakls. Start with his Cercle live set
**Monolink** - guitar and vocals over melodic techno. The one that will get her too
**Lane 8** - his *Brightest Lights* and the seasonal mixtapes
**Tinlicker**, **Yotto**, **Rone**, **Trentemoller**

## Hot tub and the fire - next to Emma Peters, Quartier Latin

Warm, no drums, works at low volume.

**Bonobo** - *Black Sands* and *Migration*
**Christian Loffler** - the melancholic end of your deep house taste
**Jordan Rakei**, **Tom Misch** - soul and jazz with a modern production
**Khruangbin** - the beach fire record. Instrumental, groovy, nothing to talk over
**Men I Trust**, **Parcels**

## The 2,206 m camp - 3 C and nothing around

Piano and strings. This is the one that will actually land up there.

**Kiasmos** - Olafur Arnalds' techno project. Ice and rhythm
**Hania Rani** - piano, Polish, extraordinary live
**Olafur Arnalds** - *Island Songs*, *re:member*
**Nils Frahm** - *Spaces*
**GoGo Penguin** - piano trio that plays like electronic music
