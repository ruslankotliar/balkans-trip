# Food strategy — activity-first (2026-06-15)

The group's rule: **time is for activities, food is mostly fuel.** Default = cook at camp (gas stove) or grab quick food; keep only a FEW special sit-down meals. See memory `feedback-activities-over-meals`.

## Per-night plan

- **D1 Tue Jun16 — Camping Krvavica (Makarska)** — `camp-cook`: Quick: Kantun Paulina ćevapi in Split (hr-kantun-paulina, ~€25-30/4, institution) covers the road lunch. Night = CAMP-COOK at Krvavica (on-site BBQ area, no restaurant, cash-only budget spot). Stop at the Promajna/Tučepi shop on the way in for groceries + buy GAS CANISTERS today in Zadar at trip start.
- **D2 Wed Jun17 — Camping Krvavica (2nd night)** — `special-sitdown`: SPECIAL #1: Konoba Feral, Brela (hr-konoba-feral-brela) — the trip's first real peka, best-rated Riviera konoba, beachside and right by the Krvavica base (no Omiš backtrack). Pre-order octopus/veal peka in the morning. Makarska nightlife = grab-and-go grill/bakery only, no second sit-down. Demote Gušti.
- **D3 Thu Jun18 — Autokamp Mungos (Mljet)** — `quick`: Quick: Mali Ston oysters shucked-to-order at roadside stalls (~€1-2 each, 30-40m) on the Ston approach BEFORE the 20:30 ferry. Arrive Mljet fed ~21:15 — too late/dark to cook, so NOT a camp-cook night. Mungos mini-market for breakfast only.
- **D4 Fri Jun19 — Home in Poplat (Stolac, rural stone house)** — `camp-cook`: Day quick: nothing committed (swim Kravica, Počitelj). Night = CAMP-COOK / self-cater at the rural Poplat house after a market stop in Čapljina or Stolac — Bosnia is the CHEAPEST grocery leg, stock up heavy here. Demote both Stolac restaurants (Han, Behar) and off-route Kočuša to options.
- **D5 Sat Jun20 — Ravna Camping (Jablanica, riverside wild pitch)** — `special-sitdown`: SPECIAL #2: Blagaj trout terraces (ba-blagaj-trout-terraces) — grilled Buna-spring trout on a terrace over Europe's largest karst spring; the meal IS the Blagaj stop, zero detour (use Fajić/Hladovina to dodge Vrelo's tourist premium). Quick: Tima-Irma ćevapi brunch in Mostar (ba-tima-irma). Night = CAMP-COOK at Ravna (no nearby restaurant); stock up at Bingo Mostar on the way out.
- **D6 Sun Jun21 — Home in Pale (BOOKED apartment)** — `quick`: City-food night, both quick institutions kept: ASDŽ sač-baked burek (ba-sarajevo-asdz, ~€3) midday + Petica ćevapi (ba-sarajevo-petica-ferhatovic, ~€6/10) for dinner. Two cheap institutions same day is fine — neither burns daylight. Booked apartment, no camp-cook.
- **D7 Mon Jun22 — Home in Boričje (Plužine, BOOKED)** — `special-sitdown`: SPECIAL #3: Etno Restoran Sočica (me-etno-restoran-socica) — rustic stone terrace over turquoise Piva Lake, lamb/veal ispod sača + kačamak, #1 in Plužine (~4.6/800+), ~€15pp, right by the bed. On a deliberately light post-rafting recovery night this slow-bake meal IS the experience. Pre-order the ispod-sača.
- **D8 Tue Jun23 — Auto Camp Mlinski Potok (Žabljak)** — `special-sitdown`: SPECIAL #4: Restaurant Or'O, Žabljak (me-oro-zabljak) — the hearty hot refuel after the 6h Prutaš summit (kačamak, cicvara, lamb under sač, trout; ~€10 mixed grill, NO pre-order needed unlike Black Lake). Earns its slot as recovery fuel on the hardest day. Black Lake restaurant demoted (needs phone pre-order hours ahead). Camp at Mlinski Potok but the body needs the hot sit-down tonight.
- **D9 Wed Jun24 — Home in Rvaši (Skadar, BOOKED 2nt)** — `special-sitdown`: SPECIAL #5: Konoba Mostina (me-konoba-mostina-rijeka-crnojevica) — Skadar carp three ways + eel fished from the river out front, in Rijeka Crnojevića where the kayak launches (zero detour). The food identity of the lake zone; eat in the same village you paddle. Hold to ~90m.
- **D10 Thu Jun25 — Home in Rvaši (2nd night, kitchen)** — `special-sitdown`: SPECIAL #6: Vinarija Mašanović, Virpazar (me-vinarija-masanovic) — 10-generation family, indigenous Vranac, €10pp tasting / €20 with platter (platter = lunch). CASH ONLY, book ≥1 day ahead. This doubles as the day's food; dinner = light CAMP-COOK / self-cater on the Rvaši terrace over the lake, no second restaurant.
- **D11 Fri Jun26 — Camping Mimoza (Stoliv)** — `quick`: Quick: BBQ Tanjga, Kotor (me-tanjga-kotor) — pick-your-meat butcher-grill, €5 plates, the Montenegro value legend, eaten between fortress and bars. Optional Moritz Eis gelato post-fortress. Stock breakfast+drinks at Voli Budva for the camp. Late party return (~02:30), no camp-cook tonight.
- **D12 Sat Jun27 — Camping Kate (Mlini)** — `camp-cook`: Night = CAMP-COOK at Camping Kate after the Cavtat boat / Pasjača swim (keeps prime afternoon daylight on the water). Konoba Ivankovski (hr-konoba-ivankovski) demoted to the ONE special-dinner OPTION — only if they pre-order the lamb peka 24h ahead and want a sit-down; it's 23min inland off the water, so it's not the default.
- **D13 Sun Jun28 — departure day (no overnight)** — `quick`: Quick only, stays in the Old-Town/kayak cluster: Barba seafood (hr-barba-dubrovnik, octopus burger ~€9, best value inside the walls, 45m) + Peppino's gelato (hr-peppinos-gelato, 15m). Light camp breakfast at Camping Kate before driving in. Glorijet is CLOSED Sundays — drop it.

## Shopping / camp-cook logistics

GAS: buy stove canisters on D1 in Zadar at trip start (Croatia, EU — reliable stock; don't rely on finding them later). GROCERY STRATEGY by price: Bosnia is cheapest — do the big self-cater shops there (Bingo in Mostar on the D5 way out for the Ravna camp-cook; market in Čapljina/Stolac for the D4 Poplat house). Croatia: smaller top-ups at the Promajna/Tučepi shop near Krvavica (D1) and a Dubrovnik-area shop for Camping Kate (D12). Montenegro: Voli supermarket (Budva D11, Virpazar/Rijeka Crnojevića) for the Rvaši-terrace self-cater (D10) and camp breakfasts. CAMP-COOK NIGHTS = 4 real cook nights: D1 Krvavica (on-site BBQ), D4 Poplat house, D5 Ravna riverside, D12 Camping Kate; plus light self-cater D10 Rvaši terrace and D13 camp breakfast. Carry staples (oil, salt, pasta/rice, coffee) bought cheap in Bosnia to avoid pricier Croatian/coastal resupply. PRE-ORDER reminders: peka at Feral (D2 morning), ispod-sača at Sočica (D7), winery booking ≥1 day for Mašanović (D10, CASH ONLY). NOT camp-cook nights (late/booked/party): D3 (late ferry arrival), D6 (booked Pale apt), D7 (special meal), D8 (post-summit sit-down), D9 (special konoba), D11 (02:30 party return)."
